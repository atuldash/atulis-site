The story

By day he built systems.
By night, songs.

ATULIS (ah-TOO-lis) spent sixteen years as an engineer in Silicon Valley, writing code that runs quietly behind apps millions of people use. The music was always there too, in late nights and notebooks.

Now it comes first. Ten songs are written and recorded, shaped by the Bay Area and the long road between two lives.
